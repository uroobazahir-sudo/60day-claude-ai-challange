SUMMARY

Workflow summary

A physical product brand launch moves through validation, positioning, naming, visual identity and packaging, go-to-market pricing, asset production, launch execution, and post-launch scaling. AI compresses the research, naming, copywriting, and visual-drafting stages from weeks to days — but packaging finalization, retail negotiation, manufacturing QA, and paid media budgets still need human judgment and real-world testing (physical samples, focus groups, retailer feedback). Treat AI output in stages 1–3 and 5–6 as a fast first draft, not a final decision.
